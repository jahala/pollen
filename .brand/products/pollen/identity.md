## Positioning

Peer messaging for agents. **umbel** fans workers out from one stem and **pleach** conducts a
plan through gates — both speak *down* the tree. pollen is the sideways channel: one agent
asks another a question and uses the answer, without the supervisor having to relay it.

Mailboxes are files on one machine (`/tmp/pollen/`), an HTTP relay across machines, and one
file of Node with no dependencies. An agent nobody has vouched for cannot simply talk to
yours: it knocks, and a human opens the gate. That gate is the product's spine — messaging
between autonomous programs is only safe when a person decides who is let in.

## Mark

Two stems leaning **apart**, each ending in an anther head, and a grain crossing the gap
between them — small as it leaves the left head, larger as it travels toward the right. The
stems are growth green (`#357E2C`); the heads and grains are anther gold (`#C8B330`).

The reading is the product: two agents that grew separately, and something carried across the
distance between them. It is drawn in the garden's diagram language — stroked stems, filled
accent heads — so it sits beside umbel's inflorescence and copeca's gauge as a sibling. The
splay is deliberate: umbel's stems *converge* on one stem (fan-out from a supervisor); pollen's
*diverge* (peers, no one above them).

Files: `assets/pollen-mark.svg` (paper) · `assets/pollen-mark-night.svg` (soil-night).

## Logo usage

- Minimum size: 16px mark height (checked at 16 · 20 · 28 · 48). Below 16, drop the small
  grain rather than shrink everything.
- Clear space: half the mark height on every side.
- Fills are exact — anther gold `#C8B330` and growth green `#357E2C` on paper; on soil-night
  the gold brightens to `#D9C44A` and the stems to `#84C56A` (the night file). Never recolour
  outside these four values.
- Never: add a bee, level the two heads into symmetry, close the stems into a V that touches,
  outline the wordmark, or set the wordmark in anything but the body face.
