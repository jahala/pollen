# pollen — colour delta

Inherits the full plotplot palette. One product claim:

- **Accent — anther gold `#C8B330`** (umbrella Product Accents table). pollen uses it for its
  mark, the bloom dot in the garden row, the `[pollen]` watch prefix, message-flow arrows, and
  fills that carry ink text (6.7:1). It is **display/fill only on paper** (1.9:1) — the same
  class as sunlight and petal.
- **Words in the accent use pollen-ink `#7E6A08`** (4.9:1 on paper) — kickers, labels, inline
  product mentions. This is the amber-ink pattern, not a second product colour.
- On soil-night both become `#D9C44A` (10.2:1) — one bright grain against deep soil.
- Status stays umbrella: **healthy `#46913C`**, caution `#B0741C`, error `#BC4126`. Gold marks
  *pollen*; green marks *delivered / allowed*; caution marks *held at the gate*. Never swap
  the two — a held message is not an error, and an allowed stranger is not a brand moment.

No other colours are added.
